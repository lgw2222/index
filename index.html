Cramorant Acquisition Guide

Find the right island, reach the required Snorlax rank, and prepare enough biscuits to befriend Cramorant.

View spawn conditions ↓

Overview

Cramorant is a Slumbering Pokémon that specializes in gathering ingredients.

SpecialtyIngredients
Sleep typeSlumbering
Main skillTasty Chance S

Spawn Conditions

Reach at least the listed Snorlax rank for Cramorant’s one-star sleep style to become available in that area.

Research areaMinimum Snorlax rank
Greengrass IsleMaster 1
Cyan BeachUltra 3
Snowdrop TundraUltra 1

Meeting a rank requirement makes an encounter possible; it does not guarantee Cramorant will appear.

Strategic Tips

  • Target Slumbering sleep. Aim for a Slumbering result during sleep research to look for Cramorant under normal encounter rules.
  • Use Cramorant Incense. If you have one, use it before a sleep session when you are ready to feed Cramorant biscuits.
  • Plan for 16 Friendship Points. Cramorant’s friendship gauge requires 16 points, so bring enough biscuits and watch for a hungry bonus.
